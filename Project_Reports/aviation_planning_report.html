Project Overview

This comprehensive aviation project analyzes the feasibility of establishing a passenger air shuttle service between Pune Lohegaon Airport (VAPO) and Mumbai Juhu Aerodrome (VAJJ). The study encompasses detailed route planning, economic analysis, regulatory compliance, operational procedures, and risk assessment for commercial aviation operations in Indian airspace.

Route Specifications

PUNE-MUMBAI AIR SHUTTLE ROUTE


Origin: VAPO (Pune Lohegaon Airport)
Destination: VAJJ (Mumbai Juhu Aerodrome)
Distance: 95 nautical miles
Flight Time: 45 minutes
Recommended Aircraft: Twin Otter DHC-6-400 (19 seats)
Cruise Altitude: 8,000-10,000 ft
Navigation: Via PUN VOR (112.9 MHz) → Direct VAJJ

Regulatory Compliance

DGCA Requirements

  • NSOP (Non-Scheduled Operator's Permit) mandatory
  • Prior Permission Required (PPR) at both airports
  • Flight plan filing for all operations
  • Security clearance for crew and aircraft
  • Insurance coverage as per regulations

Operational Procedures

  • 30-minute pre-flight planning
  • Passenger boarding and security (15 minutes)
  • Engine start and taxi procedures (10 minutes)
  • 45-minute flight time
  • Landing, taxi, and passenger disembarkation (25 minutes)

Safety & Risk Assessment

Emergency Procedures

  • Primary Alternate: VABB (Mumbai International) - 35 nm from VAJJ
  • Return Alternate: VAPO (origin airport) - 95 nm
  • Extended Range: VAGO (Goa) - 180 nm
  • Emergency Frequencies: Mumbai Radar 121.5 MHz

Crew Requirements

  • Captain with ATPL and Twin Otter type rating
  • First Officer with CPL and type rating
  • Current IFR and aircraft-specific training
  • Class 1 medical certificates
  • Regular recurrent training programs

Technical Challenges

Operational Challenges Identified:

  • Slot Coordination: Managing departure/arrival times at commercial airports
  • Weather Dependencies: Monsoon season impact on schedule reliability
  • Military Coordination: Required coordination at VAPO due to military operations
  • Daylight Restrictions: VAJJ operational limitations to daylight hours only
  • High Airport Fees: Significant impact on operating margins and pricing
  • Regulatory Compliance: Complex DGCA requirements and documentation burden

Infrastructure Requirements

VAPO (Pune) Requirements

  • Commercial terminal access arrangements
  • Ground handling service contracts
  • Fuel availability and supply agreements
  • Customs and immigration facilities
  • Maintenance hangar space

VAJJ (Mumbai) Requirements

  • General aviation terminal access
  • FBO (Fixed Base Operator) services
  • Ground transportation connections
  • Fuel services and handling
  • Limited operational hours accommodation

Project Outcomes & Conclusions

Key Findings:

  • Technical Feasibility: Route is technically viable with proper planning and regulatory compliance
  • Market Opportunity: Significant time savings (75%) for business travelers creates strong value proposition
  • Operational Complexity: Success depends on consistent demand, weather management, and regulatory navigation
  • Economic Viability: Requires premium pricing strategy to offset high airport fees and operational costs
  • Seasonal Variations: Business model must account for monsoon season impacts and demand fluctuations
